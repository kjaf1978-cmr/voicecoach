# VoiceCoach

Offline speech, voice and communication trainer (Logopädie / orthophonie / speech therapy practice) for Android phones.
Interface and training content in **English, German and French**; **professional, casual and academic** settings.

**Install:** open https://kjaf1978-cmr.github.io/voicecoach/ in Chrome on Android → tap **⬇ Install app** (or ⋮ → *Add to Home screen* → *Install*).

## Features (v3)
- **Daily plan** built each day from your voice-test weak points, due reviews, next conversation level and an unread lesson; 14-day completion calendar and streak.
- **Voice test** (read a passage + 20 s free speech) → voice profile: pace, pauses, melody, projection, clarity, fluency, median pitch; strengths and focus areas; history chart.
- **Roleplay conversations** (12 scenes × 3 languages, incl. salary negotiation, critical feedback, saying no to family, parenting, presentation opening, asking a professor for an extension). The partner speaks (text-to-speech) and **pushes back** when your answer is vague. Three levels (Guided 70 / Cued 75 / Free 80 pass mark), repeated until passed; spaced-repetition reviews (1–3–7–14–30–60 days). Every answer is **recorded, replayed and analysed** with tips; feedback covers what worked, what to improve, filler words and model phrasings. Text roleplay mode with hints.
- **Practice studio**: audio or **front-camera video** recording, import of existing files, replay with a timeline (loudness, pitch, pauses, red markers for moments to correct) and tap-to-jump tips; body-language checklist for video.
- **Articulation series check** (v3.2): several words in a row, hands-free (model pronunciation → beep → you speak → auto-advance on silence); per-word diagnosis of the wrong sound (e.g. “s sounded like t at the start”) with a specific correction hint, your recording, model and slow model playback, single-word re-practice and a repeat round for the wrong words.
- **Breath support – speak from the belly** (v3.3): guided routine “Voice from the belly” plus three measured drills – diaphragm impulses on a visual beat (count, timing, shortness, gaps, even strength), steady airflow on “ssss” (duration, evenness, support to the end) and a before/after comparison of the same sentence (loudness, pitch, sound balance, fading, melody, clarity) – each with replay and correction tips.
- **Training coach** (v3.5): every weakness detected in a recording becomes an assignment with a matched exercise (13 drills: pace, pauses, hesitations, melody, falling statements, support to the last word, projection, distortion, prompt start, fillers, clarity, voice tone, plus articulation sounds and breath support), its own practice texts, a measurable goal and a concrete next step; repeat until the goal is reached 3× in a row, then checks after 3 and 14 days.
- **Voice tone by gender & age** (v3.5): profile question on first start; age-adjusted target pitch ranges (none for children, wider during voice change, adjusted for 50+/60+); guided resonance routines (masculine: chest resonance, falling endings; feminine: forward resonance, melody, soft onsets) and a measured tone drill.
- **Learning goal & pace** (v3.5): choose what to reach and in how many days; the app estimates the workload and derives minutes per day and sessions (≤ 20 min per session, ≤ 60 min per day), fills the daily plan in sessions and shows on track / behind.
- **Speaking role models** (v3.5): built-in styles (news anchor, political keynote e.g. Barack Obama's style, TED-style talk, documentary narrator) with typical target ranges and practice texts, or import your own reference clip and compare your imitation (shadowing); gaps become training assignments. No recordings of real people are included.
- **More songs & own songs** (v4.1): Happy Birthday / Zum Geburtstag viel Glück, Jingle Bells, Row row row your boat, Hänschen klein, Alle meine Entchen, French text “Ah ! vous dirai-je, maman”; plus **own song from a recording** – import (or record) a single melody line such as your choir part rehearsal recording; the app extracts notes and phrases and trains it with the same five parameters.
- **Song training** (v4.0, Choir tab): public-domain songs with lyrics (Au clair de la lune, Ode to Joy – German Schiller / English van Dyke text, Twinkle twinkle, Bruder Jakob / Frère Jacques / Are you sleeping), transposed to your voice type, optional guide melody, count-in and karaoke syllable highlight. Five measured parameters – pitch accuracy, pitch stability, timing, sustain, breath support at phrase ends – with targets for beginner / intermediate / advanced; results per phrase, weakest phrase practised alone until in tune twice, then back to the whole song; direct links to wrong-note, long-tone, slower-tempo and breath-support drills; mastery saved per song and level.
- **Choir training** (v3.9, new Choir tab): voice-type test (glide low/high → soprano … bass, all exercises transposed to your range), choir warm-up routine, call-and-response note accuracy (five-note scale, triad, fifth & octave, thirds, long tones) scored in cents (± 30 = in tune) with flat/sharp tips and a “practise the wrong notes” loop until two perfect rounds, four-part chorale where the app plays the other three parts and scores your part per chord, and the traditional round “Are you sleeping / Bruder Jakob / Frère Jacques” with you as voice 2. Headphones recommended.
- **Word focus** (v3.8): the word with the biggest melody deviation gets a “Practise this word” drill – zoomed target curve, model with matching pitch direction, ±1.5 semitone check, 3× in a row, then back to the full sentence.
- **Melody & pace pattern trainer** (v3.7): target melody curve (from the text: stress peaks, falling statements, rising questions, pauses – or from an imported reference clip) shown as a green band with words; a cursor moves at the target pace from your first sound and your pitch appears live; afterwards pace, melody-shape similarity, pitch level and the word with the biggest deviation are scored. Built into the voice-tone card, coach drills (melody, falling statements, tone, pace, pauses, support) and speaking role models.
- **Model voice matched to your profile** (v3.6): male/female voice detection, pitch/rate fallback, manual voice picker.
- **Drills**: intonation (stress the marked word), projection (count 1–10 with rising loudness), tongue twisters scored for clarity and speed, articulation with mastery tracking, minimal pairs, reading-pace trainer, impromptu speaking, filler counter, breathing, MPT, s/z ratio, live pitch and loudness.
- **Lessons**: 9 short lessons (pauses, falling statements, melody, pace, breath support, fillers, STAR, handling pushback, body language), each with a recorded "Try it".
- **My recordings** library (stored on the phone, IndexedDB) with replay, share (WhatsApp, e-mail…) and download.
- Progress dashboard: mastery per setting/language, conversation scores, voice-score history, articulation mastery, measurements.

Speech recognition (for content scoring, fillers and clarity) uses Chrome's built-in service and usually needs internet. If the phone cannot record and recognise at the same time, the app switches to *recording + self-check of key points*. All voice analysis (pace, pauses, pitch, loudness) runs on the phone.

Not a medical device and not a replacement for a speech-language therapist.
